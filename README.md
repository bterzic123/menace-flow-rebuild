# Growth Audit — Menace

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/menace-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Menace already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in Menace's own design. Only the order and a few on-screen details change. The art direction stays the same.

On a phone, open https://bterzic123.github.io/menace-flow-rebuild/?open=proto to go straight to the prototype.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Make the close offer the weekly plan | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Let the paywall answer "how many?" | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Show their month before the price | Loader, Your month | CR +10–20% |
| 04 | Put your 4.6 stars on the paywall | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Show only the demo they asked for | Demo | CR +5–15% |

## Grounding

Every price, rating, credit rate and image comes from Menace's own App Store listing, website (menaceai.app) and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Menace.
